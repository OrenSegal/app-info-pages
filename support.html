Support

Answers to common questions. If yours isn't covered, email us — see the bottom of this page.

A podcast I subscribed to isn't showing up / isn't updating

The app fetches each show's RSS feed directly, so this is usually one of:

  • The feed URL is wrong or the show has moved to a new feed — try re-adding it from the show's current feed link.
  • The feed is temporarily unreachable — try again in a few minutes.
  • The episode hasn't finished processing yet — transcription and extraction run on-device and can take a while for long episodes, especially the first one after a fresh transcription-model download.

Episode ingestion failed or got stuck

Ingestion runs entirely on your Mac (no server on our end to check). If a single episode fails, it's usually a bad or missing audio URL in that episode's feed entry, or an interrupted download — retrying the show usually resolves it. If every episode across every show fails, check your Mac's network connection first.

The on-device speech-recognition model won't download / download is stuck

The first time you transcribe locally, the app downloads a speech-recognition model file directly from Hugging Face. If this download fails or stalls:

  • Check your internet connection and retry — the app shows download progress and will surface an error if the download fails outright.
  • A firewall or network policy blocking Hugging Face's domains will prevent this download; allow-list it or try a different network.
  • If a show's feed already includes a publisher-supplied transcript, the app uses that directly and skips local transcription entirely — no model download needed for that show.

Subscription / free tier questions

  • What's free? On-device extraction, browsing your claims, Ask, Predictions, and contradiction detection — for a small number of actively-tracked shows.
  • What does the subscription unlock? Unlimited tracked shows, bulk back-catalog backfill, and scoreboard/monthly recap share cards.
  • My subscription isn't being recognized. Restart the app first — entitlement status is checked shortly after launch and can lag by a few seconds. If it still doesn't reflect your purchase, use the "Restore Purchases" option in Settings, or email us with your purchase date.

Cloud (BYO-LLM) verification

Cloud verification is bring-your-own-model: you supply your own API key/model in Settings, and verification calls go directly from your Mac to the provider you configured. We don't run our own verification service and don't see your key or your data.
