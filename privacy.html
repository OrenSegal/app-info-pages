Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

This app is a local-first Mac app. It processes your podcast subscriptions on your device and does not operate a server, does not require an account, and does not collect analytics or usage data of any kind.

What happens on your device

When you subscribe to a show, the app downloads its episode feed and audio, transcribes it locally using on-device speech recognition, and extracts claims, predictions, and entities from the transcript — all on your Mac. None of this is uploaded anywhere. Your transcript cache and claim index are stored locally in your user data directory and are never transmitted to us, because we don't operate any servers.

Network requests the app makes

The app is not fully offline — it makes network requests directly to third parties on your behalf, never through a server we control:

  • Podcast feeds and audio — fetched directly from the RSS feed URL and audio host each show publishes, so it can transcribe episodes.
  • Publisher-supplied transcripts — when a show's feed includes a Podcasting 2.0 podcast:transcript tag, the transcript file is fetched directly from the host the publisher specifies.
  • iTunes Search API (Apple) — used for podcast discovery and subscribe search, to look up podcasts by name.
  • On-device speech model files — downloaded once, directly from Hugging Face, the first time you transcribe locally, so speech recognition can run on-device thereafter.
  • Notion API — only if you use the optional "Export to Notion" feature, and only when you trigger it. Requires you to supply your own Notion integration token in Settings; nothing is sent to Notion unless you initiate an export.
  • Reminders (EventKit, on-device, not network) — only if you use the optional "Add Reminder" export action on a claim. This stays entirely on your Mac via Apple's Reminders framework.

None of these requests pass through any server operated by us. We have no backend, no analytics SDK, and no crash reporter.

Data we collect

None. We do not collect, store, or have access to any of your data — your subscriptions, transcripts, claims, or exports never leave your Mac except when you explicitly export to Notion or Reminders, both of which go directly from your device to the third party you chose.

Third-party services

Your use of the iTunes Search API is subject to Apple's privacy policy. Your use of the optional Notion export is subject to Notion's privacy policy. Speech-recognition model downloads are static model files and do not involve any of your podcast data.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it does not collect data at all.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data flows change in a future version, this policy will be updated and the update will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
